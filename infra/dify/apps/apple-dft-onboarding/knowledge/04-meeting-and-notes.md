# Meeting prep and notes

## Before a 1:1 (5 minutes)

- Goal of the meeting (one sentence)
- Updates (3 bullets max)
- Asks (numbered)
- Risks / blockers (with options)

## After a meeting (5 minutes)

```text
Date:
Attendees:
Decisions:
Actions (owner · due):
Open questions:
My next step:
```

## Stakeholder touchpoints (senior IC)

- Manager: priorities, scope, feedback cadence
- Buddy / peer: “how we actually work”
- Cross-team partners: interfaces and SLAs
- Mentorship (optional): career and craft, not status updates

Do **not** paste confidential slide decks or proprietary specs into this assistant. Summarize in your own words at a high level if you need coaching on communication structure only.
